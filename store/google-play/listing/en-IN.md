## Short description

## Full description

ConvoCoach helps adults pause, reflect, and communicate with more clarity.

Import screenshots or enter a conversation yourself, review the extracted message sequence, and ask for coaching only when you are ready. ConvoCoach can surface limited, contestable observations, suggest respectful next steps, and create reply drafts you can edit—or ignore.

Built for user agency:

- You always review and confirm imported messages before analysis.
- Suggestions are drafts. ConvoCoach never sends messages for you.
- Interpretations state uncertainty and alternatives; they are not facts about another person.
- Safety guidance prioritizes consent, honesty, boundaries, and respectful communication.
- Raw screenshot bytes stay temporary and on-device.
- Export and deletion controls are available from Settings.
- AI responses can be reported in-app without attaching your conversation or generated text.

Your private dashboard focuses on your own choices and reflections. It does not claim to measure another person’s romantic interest, compatibility, or worth.

ConvoCoach is for adults aged 18 and older. It is not a dating service, emergency service, therapist, or source of professional advice. AI can make mistakes; review every suggestion before acting.

Free and Plus usage limits are shown in the app. Any subscription price and billing period are displayed by Google Play before purchase.
